## Proposed model (a proposal to test, not market data)
| who | price | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Riders | free | no booking fee, no surge. Price is what the rider offers and a driver accepts. |
| Drivers | one flat commission per completed ride, shown before accepting | number to set after 10+ driver interviews and unit-economics check |

Cash problem: rides are paid in cash, so commission cannot be taken from the fare. Standard answer: drivers keep a prepaid balance, topped up by a local payment method; each completed ride deducts the commission. Needs a payment partner (CIB/Edahabia gateway via an official provider) and legal advice. Not built.
