# Launch plan

**Gate before anything public:** a lawyer confirms platform licence, driver licences, insurance and data rules (see fixes.md). No public launch, no store submission, no paid ads before that. A waitlist is fine meanwhile because it only collects interest.

1. Waitlist live at /landing (built, rate-limited, tested). Riders and drivers separate.
2. Before launch: analytics and error tracking (to choose), real SMS gateway account, privacy policy listing every processor, support email and URL, app review demo account.
3. Where unhappy users talk (to confirm by reading, not scraping): Algerian Facebook groups for VTC drivers and riders, Reddit threads about ride apps in Algeria, store reviews. Join as a person, say who you are.
4. First 10 people by hand: 5 drivers and 5 riders in one city (Algiers). Talk to them, watch them use the app, ask what they would pay.
5. Beta: one neighbourhood, a small group of licensed drivers, supply before demand.
6. Public posts lead with the fix (locked price). No fake reviews, no reviewer quotes as testimonials.
7. Store submission: screenshots at current required sizes (check consoles), privacy labels, data safety form, age rating. Apple 4.1 copycat rule: show the fixes in the first screenshots and first description lines.
